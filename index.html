How We Work

A Clear Process, From First Meeting to Handover

Every project follows the same disciplined path — so nothing is missed and you always know where you stand.

1

Free Consultation & Site Visit

We visit your plot or property in Lahore, review dimensions and access, and discuss what you want to build — at no cost.

2

Requirement Gathering & Budgeting

We document your requirements in detail and prepare an itemised BOQ so you can see exactly where the money goes.

3

Design & Architectural Planning

Floor plans, elevations, 3D visuals, structural drawings, and MEP layouts — reviewed and approved by you.

4

LDA / DHA / Society Approvals

We prepare submission drawings, file the application, respond to objections, and follow up until approval is issued.

5

Grey Structure Construction

Excavation, foundation, RCC columns and slabs, masonry, and plaster — with inspections at every stage.

6

Finishing, MEP & Quality Checks

Electrical, plumbing, HVAC, flooring, kitchens, bathrooms, doors, and painting — with quality checks throughout.

7

Handover & After-Sales Support

Joint final inspection, snag list resolution, site clearance, and handover with documentation and post-handover support.
